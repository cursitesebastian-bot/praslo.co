Option B

Scroll-scrubbed video — the real product-reveal trick

The video never autoplays. Its playhead is tied directly to your scroll position — scroll down to "play" it, scroll up to reverse it, exactly like Apple's product pages.

Option A is easier and fine for ambient background video. Option B is what Apple actually uses for product reveals — it needs the video's metadata loaded before you can safely scrub it.
